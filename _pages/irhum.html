The Isotopic Reconstruction of Human Migration (IRHUM) database provides access to strontium isotope data from baseline mapping projects originally developed at the Research School of Earth Sciences, Australian National University.

The original GeoNode platform has been archived. The original datasets are preserved here and include spatial information and sample metadata, allowing users to evaluate individual samples for their specific applications.

Reference: Willmes et al. (2014), Earth System Science Data 6:117–122. DOI
