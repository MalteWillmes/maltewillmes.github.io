Pink salmon (*Oncorhynchus gorbuscha*) now occur well beyond their established range in the Barents and White Sea region, including rivers in Iceland. A key question is whether adults returning to these rivers were produced locally or are immigrants from elsewhere in the North Atlantic.

The current Iceland project uses otolith <sup>87</sup>Sr/<sup>86</sup>Sr to test whether returning adults carry natal signatures consistent with Icelandic rivers. Complementary scale analyses examine variation in marine growth and body size among regions and years.
