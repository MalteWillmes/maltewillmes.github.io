Chinook Salmon are regularly observed spawning in the Guadalupe River,
an urban tributary at the southern end of San Francisco Bay.

This project combines long-term community observations with coded-wire
tags, genetic assignment, eye-lens stable isotopes, and otolith
strontium isotope analysis to distinguish hatchery strays, natural-origin
fish, and possible locally produced adults.

The broader goal is to develop a framework for evaluating salmon
population establishment where monitoring data are incomplete and
multiple lines of evidence carry different levels of uncertainty.
