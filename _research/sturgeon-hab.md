The 2022 harmful algal bloom in the San Francisco Estuary caused extensive fish mortality, including large numbers of Green and White Sturgeon. Pectoral fin rays preserve annual growth increments and geochemical records that can be used to reconstruct age and long-term habitat use.

This project compares sturgeon collected during the bloom with fish from other periods to examine age structure and migratory histories. The analysis tests whether particular age groups or habitat-use patterns were disproportionately represented among fish affected by the event.
