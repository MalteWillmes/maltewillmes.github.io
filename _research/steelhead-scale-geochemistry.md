Otolith chemistry can provide detailed information on natal origin and migration history, but otolith collection requires sacrificing the fish. Scales offer a non-lethal archive that can be sampled from living fish and potentially used to recover some of the same life-history information.

This project develops and validates geochemical approaches for reconstructing natal origin and anadromy from *Oncorhynchus mykiss* scales. The work focuses on how faithfully scale chemistry records environmental signatures and how these measurements can be incorporated into monitoring programs where non-lethal sampling is important.
