Strontium isotope ratios vary across landscapes and can be used to trace geographic origin in both ecological and archaeological studies. In Norway, available water data are distributed across published studies, reports, and other sources, but have not yet been assembled into a consistent national baseline.

This project compiles surface-water <sup>87</sup>Sr/<sup>86</sup>Sr data from rivers and lakes across Norway and uses these records to develop a predictive isoscape. The aim is to provide a spatial reference framework for provenance applications and to improve understanding of how geology and environmental factors shape isotopic variation at a national scale.
