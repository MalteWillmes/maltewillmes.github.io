Norwegian salmon monitoring has produced large scale archives that contain long-term information on age, growth, and origin. This project focuses on digitizing these collections and making archived scale images easier to analyze and reuse.

Recent work used nearly 90,000 scale images from national archives to develop a deep-learning model for identifying escaped farmed salmon. Ongoing work is extending digital workflows for scale interpretation and measurement while retaining the value of the original long-term collections.
